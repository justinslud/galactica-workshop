How to use this queue

  • Open the proposal, read the rationale, diff, claims and evidence, then use the human review form.
  • One review per role per proposal. A duplicate role is refused; use a different role only if you can judge it.
  • A review does not approve the proposal. Approval and publishing remain separate steward actions.
  • If the base changed, the proposal is stale; publishing is refused. Open a new draft instead.
  • Do not treat simulated or same-agent reviews as independent human judgment.
